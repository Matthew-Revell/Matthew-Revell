# Matthew Revell

I build HookHour and Ribbonwren Paper Co.

HookHour is a fishing forecast that starts with the fish. Ribbonwren Paper Co. makes printable games and planners you can run in the browser and take to a printer. Both are small projects I maintain myself.

## Projects

### HookHour

A fish-first fishing forecast. Pick a fish, find waters confirmed to hold it, and get the best times, bait, and where to fish.

- Live: [matthew-revell.github.io/Bite](https://matthew-revell.github.io/Bite/)
- Code: [Matthew-Revell/Bite](https://github.com/Matthew-Revell/Bite)

### Ribbonwren Paper Co.

Printable games and planners.

- Code: [Matthew-Revell/ribbonwren-bingo](https://github.com/Matthew-Revell/ribbonwren-bingo)
